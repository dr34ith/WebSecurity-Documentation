01. Overview

Objective and methodology

Why I did this, and how I worked through it.

Objective: to deepen my understanding of web security by carefully completing PortSwigger labs and recognizing recurring vulnerability patterns, not just clearing each lab, but understanding why the underlying flaw exists and how it maps to real world findings.

The labs are structured to progressively build skill. Apprentice labs establish the fundamentals of a vulnerability class, Practitioner labs require chaining and filter evasion, and Expert labs demand multi step exploitation with minimal guidance.

My workflow centered on Burp Suite. I used the FoxyProxy extension in Chrome to toggle interception on demand, sent interesting requests to Repeater to manipulate parameters, and cross referenced everything with browser DevTools and manual HTTP request/response analysis to fully understand what was happening before moving on.
